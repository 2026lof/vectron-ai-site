Privacy Policy

Last updated: October 25, 2026

This policy explains what information Vectrons.ai ("Vectrons.ai", "we", "us") collects through vectrons.ai, our social media channels and our internal publishing tools, and how we use it. Questions: hello@vectrons.ai.

Information you give us

When you use the contact form or email us, we receive the details you provide, such as your name, email address, company, area of interest and message. We use them only to reply to you, arrange a strategy call and provide the services you ask about.

Information collected automatically

Like most websites, our hosting provider records basic technical data (such as IP address, browser type and pages visited) to operate and secure the site. We may use analytics and advertising measurement (for example Google Analytics or Google Ads conversion tracking) to understand which pages and campaigns are useful. These tools may use cookies; you can block or delete cookies in your browser settings.

Our publishing app and Google user data

Vectrons.ai operates a private internal application ("Vectrons.ai Publisher") that publishes our own marketing videos and posts to our own YouTube channel, Facebook Page and other company accounts, and manages our own Google Ads account. It signs in only to accounts owned by Vectrons.ai.

  • It uses Google APIs (the YouTube Data API and the Google Ads API) to upload our videos, read our own channel's video and comment information, reply to comments on our videos, and create and report on our own ad campaigns.
  • It does not request, collect or store data from any other Google user's account, and it is not offered to the public.
  • Access tokens are stored as encrypted secrets and used only for these purposes. We do not sell Google user data, use it for unrelated advertising, or share it with third parties except as needed to run the features above.
  • Vectrons.ai's use and transfer of information received from Google APIs adheres to the Google API Services User Data Policy, including the Limited Use requirements.
  • Access can be revoked at any time from Google Account permissions.

Social media

If you comment on or message our social media accounts, the platform (for example YouTube or Facebook) shares that content with us under its own privacy policy. We use it only to respond and to understand what our audience finds useful.

How we share information

We do not sell personal information. We share it only with service providers who help us run our business (such as website hosting, email and scheduling tools), when required by law, or with your permission.

How long we keep it

We keep enquiry and client information for as long as needed to respond, deliver our services and meet legal or accounting obligations, then delete it.

Your choices

You can ask us to access, correct or delete the personal information we hold about you, or to stop contacting you, by emailing hello@vectrons.ai.

Security

We use reasonable technical and organisational measures to protect information. No method of transmission or storage is completely secure.

Children

Our website and services are intended for businesses and are not directed at children under 13.

Changes

We may update this policy from time to time. The date at the top shows when it last changed.
